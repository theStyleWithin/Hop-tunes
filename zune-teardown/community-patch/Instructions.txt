1.) Copy: Applications (Folder)

2.) Double Click on: Shortcut to Zune in Appdata.bat 

3.) Paste: Applications (The Folder that you copied) into %SYSTEMDRIVE%\Users\%USERNAME%\AppData\Local\Microsoft\Zune (Where the batch shortcut took you, paste the "Applications" folder into that folder)

4.) Make sure Zune Software is installed and closed before going to step 5.)

5.) Double Click and Run: Enable Zune Software Features (USedit).reg (Yes to Admin Prompt, Yes to add .Reg File)

6.) Launch Zune Software and click on collections > Apps > You will see your whole library of apps ready to be synced to the Zune device)
